Que Sera Sera takes its name from the shrug — whatever will be, will be. The room answers in stone-toned monochrome, arched alcoves and candlelight: a cave built for the hours between lunch and the last call.

The kitchen is European at heart and unafraid of a detour. The bar works the same way, folding kaffir lime, jaggery and Bombay spice into drinks that still know their classics. From noon to moon, the playlist changes with the light.

WCAG 2.2 AA · RPwD Act 2016

Accessibility statement

Que Sera Sera aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA and the accessibility duties of India's Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. This statement was last reviewed on 17 November 2026.

What works here

  • Semantic landmarks, one main heading per page, and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Full keyboard access to every control, with visible focus, Escape to close dialogs, and focus trapped inside open dialogs.
  • Screen-reader announcements for form errors, booking receipts, cancellations and copy confirmations.
  • Text alternatives for images; decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Text resize to 200% and pinch-to-zoom on mobile; reflow down to 320px wide and in landscape.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 × 44px with spacing; form fields at 16px to stop mobile auto-zoom.
  • No autoplay audio and no audio-only cues. Booking is available as a text form and over WhatsApp chat.
  • High-contrast mode, larger text controls and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Directions open Google Maps, and WhatsApp opens a third-party app or site whose accessibility we do not control. If either is a barrier, call the host and we will read out directions or take the booking over the phone. Menu photography carries descriptive alt text; if an image fails to load, the text menu remains complete.

Feedback

If something blocks you, tell us and we will fix it or offer the service another way. Phone +91 77000 04311 (9am–1am daily), email accessibility@queserasera.in, or message us on WhatsApp. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

Maharashtra excise · Responsible service

Alcohol & Bar Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. These rules follow Maharashtra excise law and apply to everyone who orders or is served alcohol at Que Sera Sera.

Age limits in Maharashtra

  • 21 and over — beer and wine may be served.
  • 25 and over — spirits, cocktails and drinks containing spirits may be served.
  • Under 21 — welcome in the dining room and lounge, but no alcohol is served or allowed at the table.

The bar list stays hidden until the age check is completed. The answer is kept for this browser session only and is not linked to your booking. We may ask for valid photo ID at any point, including after ordering.

Responsible service

We serve standard measures, offer water and food with drinks, and will refuse or stop service to anyone who is intoxicated, disorderly, or buying alcohol for a minor. Staff may also refuse service where ID is unclear or appears altered. Free drinking water is always available — ask any host.

Never drink and drive. The host can call a cab or help you contact someone to collect you. Alcohol must not be brought in from outside, and unfinished bottles cannot be taken away.

Allergies and ingredients

Cocktails may contain nuts, dairy, egg, gluten, sulphites or other allergens. Tell the host before ordering and we will check the recipe or suggest an alternative. Zero-proof drinks are available to all guests.

House rules

Alcohol is served until the kitchen and bar close, at the host's discretion before our 1am closing time. We may refuse entry, service or continued presence for unsafe, abusive or unlawful behaviour. Your booking remains subject to our .
